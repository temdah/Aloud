# Aloud — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-31_

Aloud ("the app") is built to work entirely on your device. It is published by
Temirlan Dahle, operating as monkeybusiness ("we"). We do not collect, store,
or transmit your personal information.

## 1. What we collect

Nothing. Aloud has no account, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not collect
personal data about you or how you use the app.

## 2. Your documents and audio

Documents you import and the audio generated from them stay on your device. They
are never uploaded to us or to any third party. The app works fully offline.

## 3. Network use

The only time Aloud uses the network is to download the on-device voice model
files on first use (and to receive app updates through your app store or install
source). These requests download data *to* your device — they never send your
documents or personal information anywhere.

## 4. Third parties

Aloud does not share data with third parties and includes no third-party
analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## 5. Permissions

Aloud requests notification permission to show playback controls, and access
only to the files you explicitly choose to import. It does not scan your device
or read files you have not selected.

## 6. Children

Aloud is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from
anyone, of any age.

## 7. Changes

This policy may be updated in future versions of the app. The current version is
always available in the app under Settings → About.
